FLARE-AI is out! For the past 3 years, my coauthors and I have worked toward centralized, standardized AI flaw disclosure, and [FLARE-AI (Flaw Reporting for AI)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.31567) is the culmination of that effort: a [one-stop shop](https://www.ai-reports.org/) that redirects your reports to the voluntary recipient orgs working with us, rather than scattering accountability across social media, journalism, bug bounties, and feedback forms. Read the [Hugging Face blog post](https://huggingface.co/spaces/flare-ai/introducing-flare-ai), WIRED's [coverage by Will Knight](https://www.wired.com/story/flare-website-ai-flaw-reporting-safety/), and [H.R. 9333](https://science.house.gov/2026/6/h-r-9333-ai-flaw-reporting-and-security-enhancement-act), the recently introduced legislation to mandate this type of system, which some of the authors advised on. We'll be presenting FLARE-AI at [ICML 2026](https://icml.cc/) in Seoul — come talk to us!
