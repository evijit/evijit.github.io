New Hugging Face blog post: [Announcing Project ProtoStar: Making Publicly Funded Science AI-Ready](https://huggingface.co/blog/announcing-project-protostar), with Sayeed Choudhury, Greg Tananbaum, Emma Scharfmann, Georgia Channing, Eunice Mercado-Lara, and Irene Solaiman. [Project ProtoStar](https://huggingface.co/project-protostar) brings together universities, the Astera Institute, Hugging Face, and ORCA to make publicly funded datasets, models, and code easier to find and reuse for AI-driven research. It runs as a series of open challenges built on each partner institution's released scientific resources, starting with Carnegie Mellon's [Climate Risk Intelligence Challenge](https://huggingface.co/spaces/CMU-OSPO/protostar-challenge-climate-risk-intelligence) (November 2–13), which asks teams to forecast urban climate indicators for 210 U.S. cities.
