I spoke to [Science](https://www.science.org/content/article/ai-s-top-startups-are-barely-publishing-their-research) about the shortcomings of peer review as a scientific quality guarantee — peer-reviewed venues have been used effectively as marketing tools by closed products (GPT-3 at NeurIPS, AlphaFold 3 at Nature). I pointed to the current "blogification" of research and the importance of sharing code and data: reproducibility matters more than whether you publish a paper or a blog.
