I spoke to [Science](https://www.science.org/content/article/can-researchers-bring-order-confusing-world-ai-benchmarks) about efforts to bring order to the confusing world of AI benchmarks. Benchmarkers often don't fully disclose their methods, scores for the same task can vary wildly, and rerunning evaluations to check them is out of reach for most labs. The piece covers the [EvalEval Coalition](https://www.evalevalai.com/)'s work on this, including [Every Eval Ever](https://evalevalai.com/projects/every-eval-ever/), our public database of standardized, crowdsourced evaluation results, and [Evaluation Cards](https://evalcards.evalevalai.com/), which summarize how complete and reproducible an evaluation is.
