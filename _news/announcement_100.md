I spoke to [Science](https://www.science.org/content/article/can-researchers-bring-order-confusing-world-ai-benchmarks) about whether researchers can bring order to the confusing world of AI benchmarks. Benchmark results are scattered across papers, leaderboards, and model cards, reported in inconsistent formats that make them hard to compare, reproduce, or trust, which is the problem our work with the [EvalEval Coalition](https://www.evalevalai.com/) on shared evaluation reporting aims to address.
