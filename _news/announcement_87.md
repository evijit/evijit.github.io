["Position: Evaluations of AI Moral Reasoning Still Miss Half of the Picture"](https://openreview.net/forum?id=SqxApLlGP9) has been accepted to the [EvalEval Workshop at ACL 2026](https://evalevalai.com/events/2026-acl-workshop/) in San Diego! We argue that current AI moral-reasoning benchmarks largely test whether models share human values while overlooking whether they can identify and apply context-sensitive moral norms, and propose what better normative-reasoning evaluations would need.
