Dr. Avijit Ghosh is a Lead Technical AI Policy Researcher at [Hugging Face 🤗](https://huggingface.co/), a central platform for open AI models, datasets, and research tools used by industry, government, and academic developers worldwide. He is also a Research Affiliate at [the University of Connecticut](https://infothreats.cse.uconn.edu/person/avijit-ghosh/). His work sits at the intersection of AI research and policy, with a central focus on how we measure and benchmark AI systems, and whether those measurements can actually be trusted. He founded and leads the [EvalEval Coalition](https://www.evalevalai.com/), a multi-stakeholder initiative bringing methodological rigor and accountability to AI evaluation, and leads work on [Evaluation Cards](https://evalcards.evalevalai.com/), a documentation framework for AI evaluations. His broader research spans [algorithmic bias](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.02414), [agent autonomy](https://www.technologyreview.com/2025/03/24/1113647/why-handing-over-total-control-to-ai-agents-would-be-a-huge-mistake/), [AI for science](https://www.cell.com/patterns/fulltext/S2666-3899(26)00006-1), and [third-party flaw disclosure](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.07039), with coverage in [The New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2023/08/16/technology/ai-defcon-hackers.html), [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/rashishrivastava/2023/09/01/ai-red-teams-google-nvidia-microsoft-meta/), [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2024/mar/16/ai-racism-chatgpt-gemini-bias), [ProPublica](https://www.propublica.org/article/facebook-ads-can-still-discriminate-against-women-and-older-workers-despite-a-civil-rights-settlement), [Wired](https://www.wired.com/story/plaud-note-pin-ai-wearable/), and [MIT Technology Review](https://www.technologyreview.com/2025/03/24/1113647/why-handing-over-total-control-to-ai-agents-would-be-a-huge-mistake/). He represents Hugging Face in technical engagements with legislators and regulators in the United States, United Kingdom, European Union, and Singapore on open-source AI governance and evaluation standards. His work has helped shape [AI regulation in New York City](https://apnews.com/article/technology-business-race-and-ethnicity-racial-injustice-artificial-intelligence-2fe8d3ef7008d299d9d810f0c0f7905d) and prompted Facebook to [remove its biased ad targeting algorithm](https://about.fb.com/news/2022/06/expanding-our-work-on-ads-fairness/).
